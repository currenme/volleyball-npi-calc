About this tool

Developed as a research prototype for NCAA Division III women's volleyball scheduling. The probability model was fitted to 10,385 matches across the 2024 and 2025 seasons, using both teams' most recent NPI from strictly before the match date.

Model and data limitations

The monotone saturating logistic model estimates your team's probability as 1 / (1 + exp(−z)), with z = sign(d) × 4.4122334168 × (1 − exp(−|d| / 15.7237177853)) and d = your NPI − opponent NPI. Equal NPIs yield 50%. The model assumes no venue adjustment, and estimates for large NPI differences are based on relatively sparse data.

The historical archive uses the frozen reconstructed daily NPI matrices, rounded to three decimals for display. The final 2024 reconstruction matches all 419 published NPIs at three-decimal precision; some midseason values differ from official snapshots. The 2025 matrix lacks ratings for Virginia Wesleyan (Batten) and Principia. Missing historical ratings are not imputed.

Model fit and evaluation were performed on completed historical games; held-out season evaluation measures transfer, not prospective live accuracy. This is an independent research tool, not an official NCAA product.
